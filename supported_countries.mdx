---
title: "Supported Countries"
sidebarTitle: "Supported Countries"
description: "Countries you can verify passports from."
---

You can verify a passport from any country with the [Global Passport](/v3/kyc/document_verification/global/Passport), including Nigeria, Kenya, Ghana, Côte d'Ivoire, Canada, United States, United Kingdom, Australia, Burundi, China, Congo, Ethiopia, Gambia, India, Indonesia, Japan, Rwanda, Sierra Leone, Somalia, South Africa, South Sudan, Tanzania, Togo, Uganda, Zimbabwe and other countries.
