You can verify a passport from any country.

### Countries with their own passport check

- [Nigeria](/v3/kyc/document_verification/nigeria/Passport)
- [Kenya](/v3/kyc/document_verification/kenya/Passport)
- [Ghana](/v3/kyc/document_verification/ghana/Passport)
- [Côte d'Ivoire](/v3/kyc/document_verification/cote_d_ivoire/Passport)
- [Canada](/v3/kyc/document_verification/canada/Passport)
- [United States](/v3/kyc/document_verification/usa/Passport)
- [United Kingdom](/v3/kyc/document_verification/uk/Passport)

### Passports from other countries

Verify a passport from any other country with the [Global Passport](/v3/kyc/document_verification/global/Passport), including Australia, Burundi, China, Congo, Ethiopia, Gambia, India, Indonesia, Japan, Rwanda, Sierra Leone, Somalia, South Africa, South Sudan, Tanzania, Togo, Uganda, Zimbabwe and other countries.
