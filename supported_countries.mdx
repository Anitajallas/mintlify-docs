sidebarTitle: "Supported Countries"

---

You can verify a passport from any country with the [Global Passport](/v3/kyc/document_verification/global/Passport), including Australia, Burundi, Canada, China, Congo, Côte d'Ivoire, Ethiopia, Gambia, Ghana, India, Indonesia, Japan, Kenya, Nigeria, Rwanda, Sierra Leone, Somalia, South Africa, South Sudan, Tanzania, Togo, Uganda, United Kingdom, United States, Zimbabwe and other countries.
