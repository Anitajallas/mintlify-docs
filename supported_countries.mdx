### Nigeria

- **Data verification:** BVN, BVN Advanced, NIN, NIN with Face, VNIN, Driver's Licence, Passport, Voter's ID, Phone Number, NUBAN, CAC, Company Tax ID, Company TIN
- **Document verification:** National ID, Passport, Driver's Licence, Voter's Card

### Kenya

- **Data verification:** National ID, Alien ID, KRA PIN, Passport, Driver's Licence, Business Registration
- **Document verification:** National ID, Passport

### Ghana

- **Data verification:** ID Card, International Passport
- **Document verification:** Passport

### Côte d'Ivoire

- **Data verification:** National ID
- **Document verification:** Passport

### South Africa

- **Data verification:** National ID

### Uganda and Rwanda

- **Data verification:** National ID

### Canada, United States and United Kingdom

- **Document verification:** Passport

### Passports from other countries

Verify a passport from any country with the [Global Passport](/v3/kyc/document_verification/global/Passport), including Australia, Burundi, China, Congo, Ethiopia, Gambia, India, Indonesia, Japan, Rwanda, Sierra Leone, Somalia, South Africa, South Sudan, Tanzania, Togo, Uganda, Zimbabwe and other countries.
